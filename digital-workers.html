Age of Agents

A field guide to the new workforce

Digital workers, and the two wires that make them useful.

An agent on its own is just a mind in a jar. It gets real work done through two connections: one that lets it reach your tools and data, and one that lets it hand work to other agents. Those are MCP and A2A — and below, you can operate both.

Wire 01

MCP · Model Context Protocol

Give a worker its tools.

MCP is a standard socket. Any tool that speaks it — a calendar, a database, the web, your files, a code runner — plugs into the worker the same way. Tap a tool to connect it, and watch what the worker can suddenly do.

This worker can now:

    Nothing yet — plug in a tool.

    One worker, many tools. The worker always asks; the tool answers. That's the whole idea of MCP.

    Wire 02

    A2A · Agent-to-Agent

    Let workers hand off to each other.

    Some jobs need more than one specialist. A2A is a standard way for one worker to pass part of a task to another — sharing what it found, asking for what it needs. Dispatch a job and follow it down the line.

    Idle

    The team is waiting

    Press dispatch to send a task through the workers.

    No central boss — each worker takes its piece and passes the rest on. Even a worker from another company can join, as long as it speaks A2A.

    Both at once

    A real task uses both wires without you noticing.

    Say you ask a worker: “Put together this week's sales summary and send it to my team.” Here's the quiet choreography.

    1. 1
      GOAL
      You hand over the goal. One sentence. No steps, no clicking.
    2. 2
      MCP
      It pulls the numbers. The worker asks your database and calendar for this week's orders and meetings.
    3. 3
      A2A
      It calls in a specialist. A writer agent takes the raw figures and shapes a clean summary.
    4. 4
      MCP
      It delivers. The worker uses your email tool to send the finished note to the team.
    5. 5
      CHECK
      You stay in charge. It pauses for your yes before anything leaves your name.

    The smartest worker is worthless unwired. MCP gives it reach; A2A gives it colleagues.
